# Simplified SPY and QQQ benchmark parity

The strategy, SPY and QQQ ledgers receive the same actual external cash-flow list: identical amounts and dates, including zero or irregular events. Each uses the first valid raw official Close strictly after its decision/contribution date, zero transaction costs, the same fractional primary or whole-share sensitivity and the same residual-cash arithmetic.

Parity tests pass for variable events including USD 0, USD 250, USD 500 and USD 1,000. Both daily rehearsals reproduce identical cash-flow tables and parity outputs. Asset prices/actions may differ; cash availability and accounting conventions may not.

> The paper results exclude commissions, spreads, slippage, taxes, currency conversion, and broker-specific charges.

This produces a clean research comparison, not an estimate of any broker-specific realized return.
