# Variable contribution policy

Contribution amount and date are external inputs. The ledger supports no contribution, USD 250, USD 500, USD 1,000, any other positive amount, and weekly/biweekly/monthly/irregular dates.

For each event: add cash; retain the last approved portfolio/rules; compute equal target values; rank current deficits; buy at most three largest underweights at next-valid-session raw Close; do not sell merely to invest; retain residual cash. Contribution size cannot alter ranks, eligibility, entry/exit, category weights or target weights.

SPY and QQQ receive the identical event list. Fractional shares are primary mathematical units; whole shares are a simple sensitivity.

> The paper results exclude commissions, spreads, slippage, taxes, currency conversion, and broker-specific charges.
