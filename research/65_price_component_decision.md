# Price-component decision

## Decision: FAIL for standalone P4 evidence

> Exploratory survivor-biased historical Price research using a currently reconstructable yfinance universe.

| Gate | Decision | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Price implementation validity | **PASS** | exact factors/ranks, all-factor requirement, hard development cutoff and next-session-close lag; 69 tests pass |
| Development operational gate | **FAIL** | zero P4 configurations passed; lowest-turnover practical variants still required 584%–629% turnover |
| Standalone P4 versus SPY | **FAIL** | 12.85% versus 14.40% annualized in 2021–2025 |
| Nasdaq comparison | **FAIL** | 12.85% versus 15.14%; only 2 of 5 folds beat QQQ |
| Component contribution | **FAIL for P4 incremental return** | inverse volatility improved risk but reduced P3's 43.17% point return below both benchmarks |
| N=30/rank-60 | **FAIL** | 681.70% turnover and benchmark underperformance |
| Practical frequency | **Monthly least harmful** | faster review reduced return and raised turnover |
| Evidence ceiling | **INCONCLUSIVE for a true historical US universe** | all 1,069 current names are present, but inactive/delisted names and historical membership remain absent |

The exact four-factor Price component has not demonstrated standalone value.
Its inverse-volatility leg materially changes the momentum signal: drawdown is
lower, but both development and evaluation returns fall below SPY and QQQ and
turnover is operationally extreme.

P1–P3 show large survivor-biased point returns, but their 38%–41% volatility,
493%–725% turnover, suspicious source jumps and absent delisting outcomes make
them unsuitable post-result replacements. This study does not establish that
momentum generally fails; it rejects the exact frozen P4 claim under the
preregistered rules.

## Relationship to current QVP

The current Price-only and QVP top 30 overlap in exactly one name, **ENS**.
Quality and Value therefore dominate current QVP membership and can overwhelm
weak Price ranks. P4's standalone FAIL means QVP cannot borrow a claim of
historically validated Price alpha.

It does not prove QVP fails. Quality and Value may improve business strength,
valuation discipline or stability, or they may dilute Price. Their incremental
effect still requires separate historical proxies and genuine prospective
evidence. No live strategy or order is approved.
