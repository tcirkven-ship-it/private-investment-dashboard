# Simplified prospective success protocol

## Operational question

Can immutable snapshots be reconstructed; can scores be calculated consistently; are variable contributions, dividends, splits, cash and raw shares correct; and are frozen universe/portfolio rules followed? Suspend for unreconstructable data, negative cash, duplicate actions, unavailable decision inputs or unresolved ordinary split defects. A material rule/code change terminates the version.

## Selection-value question

Before emphasizing returns, report overlap and rank distance between `YF-QVP` and `YF-P`, sector/industry/name concentration, quality/value exclusions, rank persistence and turnover. Do not promote an ablation after observing forward outcomes.

## Economic question

After at least 36 months, preferably 60, report TWR, XIRR, drawdown, volatility, turnover, ending value and rolling results versus contribution-matched SPY, QQQ and `YF-P`. Retain the research objective of at least +1 percentage point annualized TWR and XIRR versus SPY and QQQ, but treat three years as preliminary rather than durable-alpha proof.

> The paper results exclude commissions, spreads, slippage, taxes, currency conversion, and broker-specific charges.

Zero-cost results are intentionally standardized paper evidence and are not realizable-return claims. Costs may be added later only as a generic sensitivity under a new version; they are not an activation gate. Paper success can justify further research discussion, never automatic real-money trading.
