# Broker-agnostic forward research protocol

The active research asks whether frozen `YF-QVP` selections and maintenance rules behave coherently and add value prospectively. It is not an execution engine.

Primary: Quality + Value + Price, N=30, equal targets, monthly ranks, retain through rank 60, quarterly corrections, existing sector/industry/position limits, and contributions toward approved underweights. YF-P/YF-QP/YF-QVGP remain ablations only.

Contribution dates/amounts are external. Paper transactions use first valid next-session raw official Close, strictly after the decision. Fractional shares are mathematical. SPY and QQQ receive identical flows/conventions. Ordinary dividends/splits are explicit; complex unresolved events pause the position for manual review without fabricated proceeds.

The historical 1m/5m/15m smoke test and IBKR-specific cost work remain preserved as superseded evidence. They are not read or required by the active experiment.

> The paper results exclude commissions, spreads, slippage, taxes, currency conversion, and broker-specific charges.

The omission is intentional. Results describe standardized paper selection/maintenance, not investor realizability.
